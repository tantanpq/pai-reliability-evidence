# PAI Open Foundation Boundary

This repository is part of the **public evidence and learning surface** around PAI. Its purpose is to share useful, reproducible reliability and assurance knowledge without publishing the whole PAI system.

## Why this boundary exists

PAI is designed around user-owned continuity, evidence, authority discipline, recoverability, and qualified learning. Some of those ideas become more valuable when shared openly. Other capabilities lose safety, privacy, or product value if raw internal implementation, private evaluation data, customer evidence, or privileged operating details are published indiscriminately.

The public boundary therefore follows a simple rule:

> Share the reusable lesson, contract, schema, example, workflow, and bounded evidence. Keep the private machinery, sensitive data, protected evaluation intelligence, and customer-specific evidence protected.

## Open Foundation candidates

The following classes are appropriate for public sharing when they are verified, sanitized, rights-cleared, and provenance-preserving:

- bounded evidence summaries;
- acceptance checklists and verification patterns;
- synthetic demos and fixtures;
- reusable skill recipes and workflow templates;
- public-safe schemas and evidence contracts;
- standards mappings and interoperability examples;
- case studies that remove private implementation details;
- negative lessons that can be shared without leaking protected failure intelligence;
- educational material about evidence-first reliability and assurance.

## Protected Core

The following are **not** automatically part of the Open Foundation:

- PAI Mind and private founder/continuity internals;
- authority, credential, trust-root, or private runtime configuration;
- private source code and proprietary implementation details;
- deep proprietary optimizations;
- protected evaluation corpora and hidden adversarial suites;
- full shared failure-intelligence datasets, repair lineage, or private causal knowledge;
- customer/tenant source, traces, incidents, exploits, logs, evidence, or product data;
- private/sovereign execution internals;
- anything whose publication would weaken privacy, safety, security, ownership, or a legitimate product moat.

## Open does not mean complete

A public pattern may describe **what to verify** without exposing every internal mechanism used by PAI to perform that verification. A public case study may show the evidence boundary and lesson without exposing private source or the complete failure corpus.

This is intentional. The goal is not to publish 100% of PAI. The goal is to make the public part genuinely useful on its own.

## Promotion rule

A private result becomes an Open Foundation candidate only when all of these are true:

1. the source Result is terminal and verified;
2. the reusable lesson is materially useful outside the original incident;
3. private source, credentials, host/path details, sensitive logs, and customer data are removed;
4. third-party rights and license provenance are clear;
5. unsupported claims are explicitly bounded;
6. publication does not expose Protected Core material;
7. the public artifact is independently readable and useful without private context.

## Relationship to PAI Assurance

Open Foundation material is community value. It can be used freely under the applicable repository license.

A full PAI Assurance engagement is a different surface. It may use private execution, customer-specific evidence, deeper evaluation, protected failure intelligence, repeated verification, or private/sovereign environments. Those capabilities are not implicitly granted or published merely because the public patterns are available here.

See `ASSURANCE.md` for the public workflow boundary and the commercial/private path.
